Boyuan Peng🧑‍🚀

I received my M.S. in Electronic and Information Engineering from Tsinghua University in 2025, advised by Prof. Peiwu Qin. Previously, I earned my B.E. in Electronic and Information Engineering at China University of Mining and Technology.

My research connects multimodal AI, generative modeling and computational imaging, including audio-visual perception, physics-guided image restoration and reliable biomedical AI. My latest project, MundiSense v1, studies observable emotional expression in speech and facial video, with explicit uncertainty.

    		MundiSense v1: Audio-visual emotion perception
    Boyuan Peng · Mundiscope
    Project / Demo / Report / Code
    2026 · Research release

    Recognizing observable emotional expression in Chinese speech and facial video. A Qwen2.5-Omni multimodal LLM adapted with LoRA works with HuBERT audio and CLIP visual specialists, followed by frozen fusion, calibration and abstention rules.

    • Multimodal LLMs
    • LoRA Fine-tuning
    • Audio-Visual Fusion
    • Uncertainty Calibration

    EmotionTalk R3: macro-F1 0.645 on 1,929 utterances, without threshold rejection. Research system; expression estimates do not establish a person’s inner state.
    		Practical guidelines for cell segmentation models under optical aberrations in microscopy
    Boyuan Peng†, Jiaju Chen†, P. Bilha Githinji†, Ijaz Gul, et al.
    Paper / PDF / Code
    2024 · Computational and Structural Biotechnology Journal

    A robustness benchmark for cell instance segmentation under simulated optical aberrations in fluorescence and bright-field microscopy. The study compares segmentation architectures and Cellpose 2.0, and introduces PLCM to identify aberration properties from point spread functions.

    • Robustness Benchmarking
    • Distribution Shift
    • Cell Instance Segmentation
    • Optical Priors

    DynamicNuclearNet + LIVECell · Optical priors · Model-selection guidelines
    		PSF-guided conditional diffusion for microscopy aberration correction
    Boyuan Peng, Jiaju Chen, et al.

    Research project

    Exploring point spread functions as physical conditioning for diffusion-based restoration of aberrated microscopy images. The goal is to recover cellular structure and boundaries while incorporating knowledge of the optical imaging process.

    • Diffusion Models
    • Physics-Guided Generative AI
    • Conditional Image Restoration

    Physical embedding · Conditional diffusion · Microscopy restoration
    		Dual-Modality Computational Ophthalmic Imaging with Deep Learning and Coaxial Optical Design
    Boyuan Peng†, Jiaju Chen†, Yiwei Zhang†, Cuiyi Peng, et al.
    Preprint / PDF
    2025 · arXiv preprint

    A coaxial optical platform combines fundus imaging and refractive sensing. Learning-based pupil localization supports alignment, while retinal vessel segmentation and multimodal learning connect optical measurements with ophthalmic screening research.

    • Multimodal Learning
    • Medical Image Segmentation
    • Learning-Assisted Optics

    Coaxial optics · Automated alignment · Retinal vessel analysis
    		Convolutional neural networks for rapid diagnosis and lesion detection of pediatric mycoplasma pneumoniae pneumonia using chest X-rays
    Jiaming Deng†, Danqing Yu†, Boyuan Peng†, Dongmei Yu†, et al.
    PDF
    Research manuscript

    ConvNeXt-Tiny distinguishes mycoplasma, bacterial and viral pneumonia from normal pediatric chest X-rays. Score-CAM highlights regions associated with model predictions, with an Android application prototype for exploring results.

    • Explainable AI
    • ConvNeXt
    • Medical Image Classification
    • Model Attribution

    Reported test accuracy: 88.20% · AUROC: 0.9218 · Four-class research evaluation
    		Intelligent proximate analysis of coal based on near-infrared spectroscopy
