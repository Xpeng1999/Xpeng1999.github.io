`mundisense-demo.gif` samples 5.3–15.0 seconds (9.7 seconds), the first EmotionTalk example. Original playback timing is preserved; frames are sampled at 10 fps and resized to 480 × 270. There is no new drawing, model output, overlay or recoloring. GIF has no audio. `mundisense-demo-poster.jpg` is a source frame at 13.8 seconds. Clicking the thumbnail opens the full demo.

The source demo replays saved model outputs with editorial presentation stages. Its architecture animation is schematic, not neural activations or inference timing. Source footage remains attributable to EmotionTalk and its original rights holders, as stated in the upstream demo README.
